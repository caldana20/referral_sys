# Refoza Brand Guide (Draft)

**Brand Idea**
Refoza is the referral OS for service businesses. It should feel confident, modern, and operationally sharp.

**Voice**
- Confident, pragmatic, and operator-focused.
- Use short active sentences and strong verbs.
- Avoid jargon, fluff, and marketing cliches.

**Palette**
- Primary Tide Teal: #0F766E
- Secondary Sunlit Amber: #F59E0B
- Ink: #0F172A
- Fog: #F8FAFC
- Sand: #FEF3C7
- Success: #059669
- Warning: #D97706
- Error: #DC2626

**Typography**
- Display: Fraunces
- Body: Space Grotesk

**Logo Direction**
- Rounded square container with a spark plus link motif.
- Use Tide Teal on light backgrounds, white on dark backgrounds.
- Keep line weight bold enough to read at small sizes.
